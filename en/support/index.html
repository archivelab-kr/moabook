How Can We Help?

Take a look at the frequently asked questions first.

Will my photos get deleted?

MoaBook never deletes photos right away. Photos you push up on the cleanup screen are just collected in the app's Trash.

They're deleted only when you tap Delete in the Trash yourself, and iOS asks you once more. Deleted photos also stay in Recently Deleted in Photos for 30 days.

I want to bring back photos from the Trash

Open the Trash from the top right of the cleanup screen or from Settings, choose photos with Select, and tap Restore to put them back. To bring them all back at once, tap Restore All Photos.

If you just put a photo in, Undo on the cleanup screen brings it right back. If you've already emptied the Trash, recover it from Recently Deleted in Photos.

What can I use for free?

Swipe to Sort, the Trash, Keep, and cleaning up groups of duplicates, similar photos, and rapid shots are all free.

You can make trip albums for your first 5 trips and 5 more after your first album, then 1 more for each day you open the app. Shrinking videos and converting Live Photos to stills can save up to 2 GB in total.

With MoaBook Premium, you can make trip albums and shrink files without limits.

How do I restore a purchase?

MoaBook Premium is a lifetime pass you buy once and keep. The price is the one shown in the App Store, and it's not a monthly subscription.

If you switch to a new iPhone or reinstall the app, open MoaBook Premium in the app's Settings with the same Apple Account and tap Restore Purchase. It comes back without paying again. Family Sharing isn't supported.

What happens when I switch to a new iPhone?

Albums, favorites, and hidden photos live in the Photos app, so with iCloud Photos on they'll be on your new device too.

MoaBook's own records, like Keep marks, Daily Stamps, and your chosen friend and items, are stored only on this device for now. They move along when you restore from an iPhone backup or transfer directly between devices.

Didn't find your answer?

Send us a message from Contact Us in the app's Settings or by email, and we'll read it and reply. Messages from the app include the app and device info we need to find the problem, and you can attach up to 3 screenshots of what you're seeing, so we can help you faster.
