Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

PolaIn short: your photos are looked at only on this device and are never sent anywhere.

Photos and Videos

Analysis such as finding trips, finding similar photos, and sorting by topic all happens on your iPhone. Photos and videos are never sent to a MoaBook server or anywhere else.

The results of your cleanup (albums, favorites, deleted photos) stay in the Photos app.

Place Names

Location coordinates and search terms are sent to Apple Maps only to look up place names for Moment Cards, trips, and photo info, or when you search for a place. The photos themselves are never sent.

Crash and Usage Data

To fix crashes and errors, and to learn which features get used, the app sends the following to Google's Firebase (Crashlytics, Analytics).

  • Error records: where in the code a crash or error happened, the error type, the screen you were on, app and iOS versions, and device model
  • Usage records: which screens and features you used (including whether you opened the purchase screen or the share sheet), and numbers like how many photos you sorted
  • App state: photo access, rough ranges of photo count and free space, and your chosen character
  • Identifiers: random numbers created when you install the app (Firebase installation ID, support ID). New ones are created if you delete and reinstall the app.

Photos, videos, album names, places, and names or contact info are never sent. Sent data is deleted according to Firebase's retention periods (Firebase privacy information).

Information Sent When You Contact Us

When you contact us from the app, we receive what you write. If you send it by email, we also receive your email address and use it to reply.

With Include App & Device Info on (it's on by default), the app also sends what's needed to find the problem: app and iOS versions, device model, language and region, photo access, photo count, free space, support ID, and recent error types. Turn it off to send only your message.

If you attach screenshots, only the images you pick are resized and attached to the email, without location or camera details.

When you send by email with Attach Trip Diagnostics File on (it's on by default), a file with only each trip's dates and times, photo counts, and distance from home is attached. It doesn't include trip names, photos, places, or location coordinates, and you can open it in Mail before sending.

We use your message only to reply and fix problems.

Purchases

Payments for MoaBook Premium are handled by the Apple App Store. MoaBook never receives payment details such as card numbers; it checks your pass using the purchase record Apple verifies on your device.

No Tracking

MoaBook shows no ads and doesn't use the advertising identifier (IDFA). Information collected in the app is never combined with other companies' data for advertising. This website also collects no visit stats and makes no requests to outside servers.

We Don't Share Your Data

We never sell or hand over the information we receive to other companies or people. The error and usage records above are processed by Google (Firebase) on MoaBook's behalf.

Contact

For questions about privacy, use Contact Us in the app's Settings or send an email.
